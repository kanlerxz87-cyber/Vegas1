How to close the route evidence gap

  1. Fix the hotel entrance, restaurant entrance, dining arrival time and return time.
  2. Use operator trip planning and dated timetables for outward and return journeys; include every walk, wait and transfer.
  3. Save the itinerary, route numbers, departure date/time and retrieval timestamp as evidence.
  4. Count a restaurant only when a qualifying bus journey satisfies the one-hour threshold. Leave unresolved routes blank rather than zero.
  5. Do not call a proposed fare change enacted without an official effective-date notice.
