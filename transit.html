Bus journeys, not a substituted travel model

Las Vegas has no subway. RTC buses are the relevant mode for the requested bus constraint; Monorail and Vegas Loop are shown only as optional alternatives, not as proof of compliance. The official planning resource is RTC journey planning.

No complete dated hotel-to-restaurant bus itinerary was obtained. A verified route map, fare or in-vehicle segment is insufficient to prove a door-to-door limit. Include access walks, waiting, transfers, dinner finishing time and the return journey. Neither 24/24 inside 60 minutes nor the derived 20-minute counts are verified.

Why the old matrix is not a booking decision

The earlier zone matrix is retained in the repository as unvalidated research, not rendered as real journey minutes. The former hotel ranking depended on those assumptions and has been withdrawn from the verified comparison (F-59). There is no claim that every hotel is within an hour of every restaurant.

RTC journey planning supplies the discovery path. Record exact origin/destination, service date, departure/arrival time and each leg before filling any hotel count.

Do not mix fare products

RTC residential regular single fare is $2; $1 is reduced fare, not the visitor default. The Monorail’s separate $1 local fare requires eligible Nevada ID and has purchase limits. For electronic Monorail tickets, 10 singles cost $57.50, below the $59.95 seven-day pass; 11 singles cost $63.25. That is fare arithmetic only—not a recommendation that this couple will use 11 rides, or that stations satisfy the restaurant walking/bus constraint.
