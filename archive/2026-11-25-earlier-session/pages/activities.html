Transportation options in Las Vegas:

  • RTC Deuce Bus: 24-hour service on the Strip. $6 for 2-hour pass, $8 for 24-hour pass.
  • Las Vegas Monorail: East Strip (Sahara to MGM Grand). $5.75 single ride (online), $6.25 at station. $13 for 24-hour pass.
  • Rideshare (Uber/Lyft): $12-25 for typical Strip trips.
  • Walking: Free between adjacent Strip properties.
  • Free trams: Between Mandalay Bay, Luxor, Excalibur; and between Park MGM, ARIA, Bellagio, Crystals.

Important: Las Vegas has no subway or underground metro system. Public transportation is bus-based (RTC) with a Monorail on the east Strip.
