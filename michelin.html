🍽 Dining plan for a couple

A cost-aware way to sample the stars without overspending.

  • Best value star experience: L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon (~$510 for two) — a one-star tasting menu at roughly half the price of the two-star room next door.
  • The splurge: Restaurant de Joël Robuchon (~$1,050 for two, before wine) — the only Two-Star in the Southwest.
  • Most approachable: the three Bib Gourmands (China Poblano ~$62–100 for two; Esther's Kitchen & YEN Viet Kitchen cost pending verification) — "good food at good value," the guide's own value tier.
  • Tip: é by José Andrés tickets go on sale three months ahead and are prepaid/non-refundable — book at the 3-month mark from your travel date. Source: ebyjoseandres.com.
