Review result

The site is useful as a source-linked research dashboard, not yet a booking-ready optimized vacation. The biggest inherited irregularities were unsupported hotel/transit rankings and headline rates, not HTML build failures. Every source dataset remains available in Git; the new view prevents incomplete evidence being promoted into facts.

Checks cover schema/evidence consistency, local links and anchors, root/docs parity, required sources, blank unsafe decision fields, escaping and responsive markup. They do not certify external websites, future availability or prices. Publication/deployment results are recorded in the coordination board after merge.
