Hotel Ranking by Michelin density (within 1 hour)

Hotels ranked by the number of Michelin-distinguished restaurants reachable within 1 hour by public transit (RTC Deuce bus / Las Vegas Monorail) or on foot, with a tie-breaker on walk-time to the 4 starred restaurants and total cost.

Irregularity — no subway in Las Vegas

Las Vegas has no underground metro/subway system as of 2026. The "1 hour by subway or bus" criterion is therefore fulfilled using the RTC Deuce bus (24-hour Strip bus) and the Las Vegas Monorail (7 stations, Sahara → MGM Grand, ~13 min end-to-end). Sources: appsavvytraveller.com · nomadotravel.app.

Methodology: From any central-Strip hotel, all 24 Michelin restaurants are reachable within 60 minutes. The differentiator is walk-time to the 4 starred restaurants (Joël Robuchon, L'Atelier, Guy Savoy, é by José Andrés) plus total 7-night cost. "Starred within 15 min" = the count of the 4 starred restaurants reachable within ~15 min by walk/ride.
