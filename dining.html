The monorail does not reach the airport or downtown; the RTC Deuce does and runs 24 hours, which matters if Fremont Street and Chinatown are in the plan. No seven-day RTC pass was verified — the arithmetic above stacks three-day passes.

To turn this into a trip total

  1. Add the room. Use the property's own checkout for the exact dates: named room, two adults, seven nights, mandatory resort fee and taxes, cancellation terms, timestamp (Hotels, F-58).
  2. Add airfare and airport transfers — neither is in this dataset.
  3. Add gratuity and wine. Partage's published wine pairings are $60–$108 per person; the other venues publish no pairing price, so none is estimated.
  4. Keep the nights flexible until the room is quoted. No universal cheapest week is established (Q-06).
