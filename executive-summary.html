Key findings

  • February is the value month — but Feb 14 (Super Bowl + Valentine's) and Feb 15 (Presidents Day) spike prices. Arriving Feb 16–18 avoids the worst of it. Details →
  • Exact Feb-2027 hotel prices are not published yet on official sites. This site leaves them blank + UNVERIFIED rather than inventing numbers, and gives you the formula + calculator.
  • Joël Robuchon publishes no prices — its cost-for-2 is flagged UNVERIFIED (third-party reports only). Hours are official: Tue–Sat 5:00–9:30pm, closed Sun–Mon.
  • All 4 starred restaurants are on/near the Strip, so any Center-Strip hotel keeps every starred meal ≤1 hr by Deuce/Monorail. Off-Strip Bib gems (Esther's Kitchen, Yen Viet Kitchen) need a bus transfer — still ≤1 hr from Center Strip.
