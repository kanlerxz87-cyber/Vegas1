What "MICHELIN" means here

Las Vegas had no MICHELIN Guide between 2010 and 2025. The 2026 Southwest selection — announced 31 August 2026 on the official michelin.com press release — is the city's first since 2009, which is why so many videos and blogs still describe restaurants as starred when they no longer are.

Read this before budgeting. Every figure is before tax, gratuity and wine unless the venue prints otherwise; a tasting-menu quote does not include drinks, and several venues add automatic gratuity to tasting menus. Venues marked closed are priced for a service that is not running. Where a venue prints no menu price the cell says on request — that is the honest answer, not an average.

Two consequences for planning. First, the starred rooms are the most expensive nights and not all of them publish prices, so reserve before booking the room (S-04). Second, the cheapest MICHELIN-recognised dinner on this list is SoulBelly BBQ at $80 for two, and several Bib Gourmand / Recommended rooms land under $200 for two — a stars-only week is a choice, not a necessity.
