What "MICHELIN" means here

Las Vegas had no MICHELIN Guide between 2010 and 2025. The 2026 Southwest selection — announced 31 August 2026 on the official michelin.com press release — is the city's first since 2009, which is why so many videos and blogs still describe restaurants as starred when they no longer are.
