Executive Summary

A verified plan for a couple's 1–2 week Las Vegas vacation between February 15 and March 23, 2027, prioritizing the preferred Feb 15–18, 2027 window.

The trip in one paragraph

Base the trip at a central-Strip hotel with strong access to Las Vegas's 24 Michelin-distinguished restaurants from the inaugural 2026 MICHELIN Guide American Southwest. Four of those restaurants currently hold Michelin Stars — Joël Robuchon (★★), L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon (★), Restaurant Guy Savoy (★) and é by José Andrés (★) — and all four are clustered on the central Strip, within a short walk or ride of each other. A 7-night stay is the planning unit; MGM Grand or Park MGM give the best Michelin access, and the cheapest bookable window is the Mar 1–8, 2027 weekday stay if dates are flexible.
