F-29Hotel price conclusions conflict across source and capture dates

The older Booking.com sample and official-engine notes from 2026-11-25 do not agree on a universal cheapest week. A fresh official-engine check on 2026-11-26 found property-specific rates: the same named Horseshoe King category was lowest on 15–22 Mar among five quoted windows ($105/night). At Caesars Palace the named standard King category was quoted at $201/night for 15–22 Feb and $317/night for 15–22 Mar; two other pages showed a different hearing-impaired King variant and were left blank for like-for-like comparison, and 1–8 Mar returned no exact quote. No city-wide or universal cheapest week can be supported by this sample. Direct quotes are volatile and exclude tax.

Next step: Compare the exact room category and dates on the hotel's own checkout page immediately before booking; do not rely on the old 'March is cheapest' or 'February is cheapest' headline. For a valid comparison, match the named room category as well as the dates.

F-30Resorts World rate page contains contradictory resort-fee legends

A nightly calendar value cannot safely be converted to a comparable seven-night stay price until the legend is resolved.

F-31Private bathroom requirement not stated on the live direct-rate cards

Current official quote cards name a bed category and describe a bathroom for Horseshoe and Caesars; they do not explicitly state that the bathroom is private. Room assignment rates at Flamingo do not guarantee a bed category at all. The bed and private-bath requirement must be confirmed together against the exact booked category.

Next step: Confirm the exact room's bed and private bathroom in writing before payment. Do not select an unassigned room rate for this brief.

Decision-critical open points

  • Confirm trip length: preferred dates 15–18 Feb are three nights, while the main hotel comparison uses seven-night blocks.
  • Confirm the exact room category has a queen/king bed and a private en-suite bathroom before payment; avoid unassigned-room offers.
  • Request a full checkout quote including taxes, resort fee, and cancellation terms; the page's nightly-rate arithmetic is not a final bill.
  • Choose whether price or Michelin proximity matters more. The requested ≤60-minute metric is 24/24 for all hotels and does not distinguish them.
