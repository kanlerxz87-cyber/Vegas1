The page describes a 72-hour cancellation rule with a one-night room-and-tax penalty and a one-night deposit at booking. Availability, final taxed total and the exact rate’s full terms still need confirmation. An advertised sale deadline does not establish that booking now is cheaper than booking later.

  • One named queen-or-larger room with an in-room/private bathroom; two adults; seven nights.
  • Room + mandatory resort/property fees + taxes in the same currency, with refundable/cancellation terms recorded.
  • No multiplication of a rounded average-nightly display into an exact stay total; no blanket tax rate or resort-fee assumption.
  • Restaurant and activity subtotals for two are separate from final all-in amounts.
