Real Pricing

Total costs for 2 adults: hotel stays are shown as the total for a 7-night stay using actual dates, with the cheapest verifiable upcoming booking window. Restaurant costs are totals for two. Every figure links to an official source.

How prices are reported

Hotel: total for 7 nights (room rate × 7, plus any verified mandatory resort/destination fees where published). Restaurant: estimated total for two adults (food; tax/tip noted where published). Where an exact figure is not published, a verified range or blank+flag is used — never guessed.

Notes & caveats

Caveat: Hotel and restaurant prices are dynamic and date-specific. The values here are the best verifiable figures captured at research time for the stated dates; confirm live on the official booking/menu pages before committing. Tax (LV ~8.38% state + local) and gratuity are extra unless noted.

Sources: hotel official booking pages and restaurant official/menu pages.
