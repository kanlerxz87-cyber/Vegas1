? Questions to answer before deciding

A clear list of the questions, the information we already have, and the information we do not have. Decisions should wait until these are resolved.

① Trip scope & budget

1. Which exact dates, and how many nights?
Have: window 2027-02-15 → 2027-03-23; preferred 2027-02-15 → 2027-02-18; hotel pricing verified for a 7-night stay (02-15 → 02-22). Don't have: the final arrival/departure dates and whether 3, 4 or 7 nights is preferred. Needed: confirmed dates + night count to lock the booking and re-price.
2. What is the total trip budget (lodging + dining + activities + shows + transit)?
Have: lodging $556–$1,210 for 7 nights; Michelin dining ~$62–$1,050 per couple per meal. Don't have: a budget ceiling or how many "splurge" starred meals to include. Needed: a total budget and a dining allocation to shortlist hotels and restaurants.
3. Is a guaranteed queen-or-larger bed a hard requirement?
Have: MGM Grand (Studio King) and Luxor (Pyramid King) confirm a king bed. Flamingo, Horseshoe and Planet Hollywood list "Room Type Assigned on Arrival." Don't have: confirmation that the assigned room will be a king. Needed: decide whether to accept the risk (lower price) or require a confirmed king (MGM Grand / Luxor, or contact the hotel to guarantee).

② Hotel & dining decisions

4. How to break the hotel tie?
Have: all five hotels reach ≈23 of 24 Michelin restaurants within 1 hour (the Deuce covers the Strip), so the primary ranking metric is effectively tied. Don't have: a weighting between price, walkability to starred restaurants, and hotel quality. Needed: a priority order (e.g., "cheapest that still reaches everything" vs "best on-site dining access") to finalize the pick.
5. Are resort fees included in the verified hotel prices?
Have: Booking.com states the 7-night totals "include taxes and fees." Don't have: confirmation of each property's separate mandatory resort fee (common in Las Vegas and often charged at check-in, not always included in the rate). Needed: check each hotel's resort-fee policy to get the true all-in lodging cost. UNVERIFIED
6. How many and which starred meals to book?
Have: costs for the four starred restaurants (~$510–$1,050 for two). Don't have: how many splurge meals to reserve, and reservation availability for the chosen dates. Needed: dining priority + reservation timing (é by José Andrés releases tickets 3 months ahead and is prepaid/non-refundable).
7. Exact current prices/hours for the 17 "Michelin Selected" restaurants?
Have: distinction status and addresses for all 24; verified prices/hours for only the starred ones + China Poblano + Stubborn Seed. Don't have: current menu prices and hours for the rest. Needed: confirm each via its official site / Resy / OpenTable before relying on them. UNVERIFIED

③ Logistics & activities

8. Rely on transit/rideshare, or rent a car?
Have: Deuce (24/7, full Strip + Downtown) and Monorail (east Strip) cover all listed activities within 1 hour. Don't have: a decision on whether to rent a car — relevant for off-Strip dining (Anima by EDO) and day trips (Red Rock Canyon, Hoover Dam) that exceed the 1-hour transit budget. Needed: decide if day trips are in scope; if so, plan a rental car.
9. Preferences for Strip vs Downtown vs Chinatown exposure?
Have: options in all three areas, all within 1 hour of the hotels. Don't have: a stated preference for nightlife/shows vs neighborhood dining. Needed: a rough "style" preference to shape the itinerary.
10. Shows / nightlife interests and budget?
Have: Cirque "O" (~$105–180), STRAT, High Roller, etc. Don't have: interest level, show budget, or preferred showtimes. Needed: a shortlist of shows to price and reserve.

④ Suggestions to discover the missing information

Concrete, low-effort actions to close the gaps above — do these before finalizing any decision.

  • Price the whole window, not just the preferred dates. Re-run the Booking.com search for each date range across 2027-02-15 → 2027-03-23 to find the true cheapest booking window. (Closes Q1, Q2.)
  • Confirm resort fees. Check each hotel's official site / terms for the mandatory resort fee and add it to the 7-night total. (Closes Q5.)
  • Guarantee the bed. Email/call Flamingo, Horseshoe and Planet Hollywood to request a confirmed king bed, or default to MGM Grand / Luxor. (Closes Q3.)
  • Verify transit times. Run each hotel→restaurant/activity pair through the RTC trip planner or Google Maps to replace the estimates with exact one-way times. (Closes Q4, Q8.)
  • Confirm Selected-restaurant details. For each of the 17 Michelin Selected restaurants, pull current hours and menu prices from the official site / Resy / OpenTable. (Closes Q7.)
  • Set reservation reminders. Calendar alerts at the 3-month mark for é by José Andrés and at the booking window for the starred tasting menus. (Closes Q6.)
  • Collect official social handles. Search each restaurant/activity for its official Instagram/YouTube/TikTok and add to the tables. (Improves social coverage.)
