S-01 Compare exact dates on the hotel's own booking engine before shifting the trip high impactrecommended

Do not assume 8–15 March is cheaper overall. The 25 Nov Booking.com snapshots showed lower March quotes for The Venetian, Palazzo and Cosmopolitan. On 26 Nov the Venetian's figures were not reproduced on Booking.com's own page (F-39), and the Palazzo and Cosmopolitan figures have not been re-checked (F-44). A separate official-property-engine sample reports the preferred 15–22 February week as cheapest at the properties it checked. The samples differ by property coverage, room/rate terms and fee/tax basis. This evidence does not establish a market-wide cheapest week.

Trade-off: Keeping the preferred dates avoids changing plans based on a source conflict; checking exact dates and the same room/fee basis property by property may still identify a cheaper alternative. Recheck rates close to booking because the captures are dated 25 November 2026.

Evidence: Booking.com snapshot table: data/price-windows.json and data/hotels.json. Official-engine sample with per-property booking links: docs/session-af795000/hotels.html.

S-02 Decide whether this is a 3-night or a 7-night trip high impactneeds your decision

The brief says 1–2 weeks but the preferred dates 2027-02-15 → 2027-02-18 are three nights. Every hotel total on this site is for seven nights. If the real plan is three nights, the hotel line is roughly 43% of what is shown.

Trade-off: Three nights cannot cover 21 Michelin restaurants plus the museums — realistically it covers four or five restaurants. Seven nights is the shortest trip where the list is plausible.

Evidence: data/questions.json Q-02

S-03 Refuse 'Room Type Assigned on Arrival' rates medium impactrecommended

The three lowest Booking.com quote snapshots (LINQ $564, Flamingo $626, Horseshoe $717) are all unassigned-room promotions. These offers do not guarantee the queen-or-larger bed the couple asked for, and the quote totals are not official property rates.

Trade-off: A named king or two-queen room is necessary to verify bed fit. The price difference is not established here; check the named room on the property's official booking engine rather than adding an assumed uplift.

Evidence: data/hotels.json flags F-11 and F-29; official-engine examples are linked at docs/session-af795000/hotels.html

S-04 Book the four starred restaurants first, then build the trip around them high impactrecommended

é by José Andrés has nine seats and opens bookings about three months out; Joël Robuchon, L'Atelier and Guy Savoy are dinner-only and dark on specific weekdays. Reservation order should be é → Robuchon → Guy Savoy → L'Atelier, then fill the unstarred list around whatever dates remain.

Trade-off: Locking the starred nights first means the hotels must be chosen afterwards — the reverse of how people normally book.

S-05 Use the Monorail 7-day pass, not single rides medium impactrecommended

A single Monorail ride is $6.25 paper or $5.75 with an e-ticket; the 7-day pass is $64 paper or $59.95 electronic. Ten rides break even at paper prices. Over seven nights the pass is the cheaper and simpler option, and it covers MGM Grand ↔ SAHARA, which is exactly the Michelin corridor.

Trade-off: The Monorail does not reach Chinatown or downtown, so RTC buses are still needed for roughly half the restaurant list. The pass is not a substitute for bus fares.

Evidence: data/transit.json

S-06 Base the trip centre-Strip, not at the Venetian or the Wynn medium impactneeds your decision

The Venetian complex holds four Michelin listings and the Wynn holds one, which makes the north Strip look like the obvious base. But the centre Strip is within 20 minutes of 15 Michelin restaurants — more than any other zone — and is reachable from both ends of the Monorail.

Trade-off: Choosing centre Strip (Cosmopolitan, ARIA, Bellagio, Vdara, Caesars) gives up the in-building convenience of four restaurants under one roof. Price comparisons should use identical dates, room classes, taxes and resort-fee basis from official property engines; the current OTA/direct samples are not comparable enough to establish a cost premium.

Evidence: data/transit.json, data/hotels.json

S-07 Pair the museums with the downtown restaurants on one day medium impactrecommended

The Mob Museum, the Neon Museum, Fremont Street, Esther's Kitchen and SoulBelly BBQ are all inside the downtown/Arts District zone — which is the only zone that is far from everything else. Doing them on a single day turns a 30-minute trip each way into one trip.

Trade-off: It consumes a whole evening, so a starred dinner cannot happen that night. Slot it on arrival day.

Evidence: data/transit.json, data/activities.json

S-08 Budget resort fees and 13.38% tax explicitly medium impactrecommended

The Booking.com totals this site quotes exclude the resort fee and the local room tax. On the Strip that is typically $35–$55 per night plus 13.38% — roughly $250–$390 extra on a seven-night stay, which is a whole tasting menu at Anima by EDO.

Trade-off: No downside; it is purely an accounting correction. The Wynn rows already show both bases side by side as a worked example.

Evidence: data/questions.json Q-09

S-09 Treat Black Friday rates as a floor, not a forecast low impactrecommended

Several of the cheapest totals captured on 2026-11-25 carry a 'Black Friday Deal' label — LINQ, Flamingo, Golden Nugget, Bluegreen, Cosmopolitan, Bellagio and Fontainebleau all show one. They expire long before 2027 and will not be reissued at the same depth.

Trade-off: Planning against the promotional number risks a 30–50% shock when the real 2027 rate appears. Use the non-promotional 'original price' figure as the conservative planning number where one is printed.

Evidence: data/hotels.json
