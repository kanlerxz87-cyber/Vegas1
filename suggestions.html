Suggestions (opinionated, clearly labeled)

1. Dates

Suggestion: If the preferred Feb 15–18 window is a hard constraint, book the 3-night Flamingo at $166.65 before tax (free cancellation until Feb 13, 2027) and decide by January whether to extend to the full 7 nights (Feb 15–22, $625.65) or shift the whole trip to Feb 22–28 (MGM Grand drops to $1,293.60 — post–Super Bowl dip).

Why: Presidents' Day (Mon Feb 15) + the day-after–Super Bowl surge (game Sun Feb 14) make the first half of your preferred week peak-priced; cancellation windows mean you don't have to choose today.

3. Restaurant booking order

  1. First (release date): é by José Andrés — 8 seats, prepaid, releases ~3 months out; set an alert.
  2. Second: Restaurant de Joël Robuchon (2★) + Guy Savoy (1★) — formal rooms book out for weekend seats.

  4. Anytime: Bib Gourmands (Esther's, China Poblano, YEN Viet) and Chinatown listed spots — walk-in friendly except weekends at Sparrow + Wolf.

4. Money-saving moves (all verified)

  • Book the Black Friday rates now (−24% to −50%, live through Dec 31, 2026) with free cancellation until Feb 12–13, 2027 — then re-price in January.
  • Take the $8 24-h transit pass on Deuce days you ride more than twice; $35 7-day pass if you ride daily.
  • Milos lunch 3-course $45 pp vs $220–380 dinner for two — same kitchen.
  • Esther's / YEN Viet / SoulBelly / China Poblano deliver Bib-level meals for $30–130 for two — sprinkle these between splurges.
  • MGM's $665.91 3-night rate includes a $100 food/drink credit — better value than the bare $614.10 non-ref if you'll eat on-site.

5. Transit strategy

There is no subway. Base everything on: walking (center-Strip cluster), Deuce (Strip ↔ downtown, 24 h), Route 203 (Chinatown), and the Monorail (east-Strip hotels). One 7-day RTC pass ($35) covers the Deuce/SDX/Route 203 legs in this plan. Keep a rideshare fallback for the Neon Museum (north edge of the Deuce route).
