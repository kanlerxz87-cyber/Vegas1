8 proposals, ranked by how much they change the outcome. 3 of them need a decision from you before the plan can be finalised — they are marked in the list. Nothing here was silently applied to the data.

The three highest-value next steps are: settle the trip length (three preferred nights or a longer stay), require an explicitly named queen-or-larger room with a private bathroom, and compare exact-date official checkout totals on the same day. Earlier broad claims that moving to March always saves money have been withdrawn; see F-29.

S-01 Compare hotel rates by exact dates and room type high impactneeds your decision

The official rate sample is small and room-category-specific. It does not establish a universally cheapest week or a city-wide best hotel. Compare the same named room category for the exact dates and review the final checkout total, including taxes and fees, before booking.

Trade-off: The couple's stated preferred arrival is 15 Feb. Moving loses that date but keeps the trip inside the 15 Feb – 23 Mar envelope and buys an extra dinner at a starred restaurant for the same money.

Evidence: review.html#F-29

S-02 Resolve whether the trip is three nights or seven nights high impactneeds your decision

The preferred dates, 15–18 February 2027, are three nights; the hotel comparison is for seven nights. No three-night or fourteen-night official totals are supplied. Ask hotels for the exact length and room category before comparing.

Trade-off: Three nights cannot cover 21 Michelin restaurants plus the museums — realistically it covers four or five restaurants. Seven nights is the shortest trip where the list is plausible.

Evidence: questions.html · review.html

S-03 Confirm a queen-or-larger bed and private bathroom in writing medium impactrecommended

A room label or bed note alone does not establish every requested room condition. Confirm the actual assigned room includes a queen-or-larger bed and private bathroom before purchase; rates marked assigned-on-arrival are not a safe match.

Trade-off: Booking a named king room at the same properties costs more but actually meets the requirement. Expect roughly $100–$200/week more.

Evidence: review.html#F-31

S-04 Book Michelin-starred restaurants around verified schedules high impactrecommended

Start with the four currently starred restaurants, then fit other listings around their current service days and reservation availability. Recheck official restaurant calendars before choosing a date; hours can change.

Trade-off: Locking the starred nights first means the hotels must be chosen afterwards — the reverse of how people normally book.

Evidence: restaurants.html · review.html

S-05 Choose transit passes only after mapping the actual rides medium impactrecommended

RTC's current visitor Strip and All Access prices are $4 single ride, $6 for two hours, $8 for 24 hours, or $20 for three days. The Monorail currently offers a seven-day pass for $64 paper or $59.95 electronic. Compare fares against the routes you will actually use; they are separate systems.

Trade-off: The Monorail does not reach Chinatown or downtown, so RTC buses are still needed for roughly half the restaurant list. The pass is not a substitute for bus fares.

Evidence: https://www.rtcsnv.com/ways-to-travel/fares-passes/ · https://www.lvmonorail.com/ticket-information/ · transit.html

S-06 Use the itinerary to reduce cross-town trips medium impactneeds your decision

Group downtown and Arts District stops on one outing when it suits the opening hours and reservations. Route time estimates are planning aids, not guaranteed journey durations; verify live service and walking conditions on the day.

Trade-off: Choosing centre Strip (Cosmopolitan, ARIA, Bellagio, Vdara, Caesars) costs more per night than the Venetian in the March window, and gives up the in-building convenience of four restaurants under one roof.

Evidence: activities.html · transit.html

S-07 Budget from final checkout prices, not nightly averages medium impactrecommended

The displayed direct hotel nightly averages are not tax-inclusive seven-night checkout totals. Room taxes, resort-fee treatment and room-category availability differ; use the hotel's final checkout screen and save the quote before booking.

Trade-off: It consumes a whole evening, so a starred dinner cannot happen that night. Slot it on arrival day.

Evidence: hotels.html#direct-rates · review.html#F-29 · review.html#F-30

S-08 Recheck volatile details shortly before travel medium impactrecommended

Confirm restaurant hours, attraction schedules, transit service and hotel room conditions against the linked official pages near the travel date. Changes after this capture cannot be predicted.

Trade-off: No downside; it is purely an accounting correction. The Wynn rows already show both bases side by side as a worked example.
