Four things that change the answer

  1. The 7-night basis collides with the requested dates. The preferred window 2027-02-15 → 2027-02-18 is three nights, not seven. Seven-night totals starting on 15 Feb are used so the figures match the brief — Q-02.
  2. The "within 1 hour" ranking does not rank anything. Every hotel in Las Vegas is within an hour of all 24 restaurants, so the metric returns 24/24 for all 32 properties. A 20-minute column was added and is what actually separates them — Q-01.
  3. One ranked hotel no longer exists under the name this site priced. The official page for The Cromwell now redirects to The Vanderpump Hotel, so that row's $1,978 total is a Cromwell-era number and must be re-quoted before it is used — F-47.
  4. The cheapest week is not settled across sources. The 25 Nov Booking.com snapshots showed lower March quotes for some large resorts, but the Venetian figure was withdrawn on 26 Nov (F-39). A separate 25 Nov sample of official booking engines found the preferred 15–22 February week cheapest at properties checked. The samples are not like-for-like. Compare exact dates and named-room terms at the hotel's own booking engine before moving dates — Q-06.
