Three things that change the answer

  1. The 7-night basis collides with the requested dates. The preferred window 2027-02-15 → 2027-02-18 is three nights, not seven. Seven-night totals starting on 15 Feb are used so the figures match the brief — Q-02.
  2. The "within 1 hour" ranking does not rank anything. Every hotel in Las Vegas is within an hour of all 24 restaurants, so the metric returns 24/24 for all 32 properties. A 20-minute column was added and is what actually separates them — Q-01.
  3. The cheaper-week claim is unproven. The 25 Nov captures suggested 8–15 March is cheaper for the big resorts. A 26 Nov check of the Venetian's own Booking.com page did not reproduce it, so the figures are withdrawn — F-29, Q-06.

If you read only one row

Desert Rose Resort — $922 for seven nights, 9.1 guest score, a 646 ft² one-bedroom suite with a full kitchen, a king bed and a private bathroom, no resort fee, and a 15–20 minute walk to the Joël Robuchon and L'Atelier rooms at MGM Grand. It is the only property that is simultaneously cheap, well-rated and correctly bedded. The catch is that it is a low-rise condo hotel with no casino, set one block off the Strip — Hotels explains the trade.
