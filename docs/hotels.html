Result: $553.58 cheaper (25.2% less) than the preferred February window, for the identical room. The four arrival dates span $636.06 from cheapest to dearest. The preferred window is not expensive because February is a peak month. It is expensive because of two specific nights: $348.75 on each of Tuesday 16 and Wednesday 17 February, both above any Friday or Saturday in the same window. The cause of those two nights is not something this site can evidence, and no convention calendar was checked — so the page does not claim one.

What this comparison does not establish
  • It is ONE property. It does not show that March is cheaper market-wide; other properties may price differently.
  • It is ONE capture moment. Rates move daily, and a promotional rate valid today may not exist tomorrow.
  • It does not prove booking earlier or later will be cheaper — no purchase-timing evidence exists (F-58).
  • The 8 March window is still inside the brief's 15 Feb - 23 Mar travel window, so this is a within-brief substitution, not a different trip.
  • It does not account for convention calendars, which can invert the ranking at other properties on the same dates.

How to produce a defensible ranking

  1. Reject run-of-house offers without guaranteed queen/king bed and private bathroom.
  2. Compare like-for-like official all-in checkout totals, room terms and cancellation deadlines for two adults.
  3. Rank first by count of verified bus journeys ≤60 minutes at intended dinner times, then by total price; amenities remain a separate review criterion until preferences are known.
  4. Include walking, waiting, transfers, return journeys and February/March closures. A zone estimate is insufficient.
