  • Caesars Palace: the official Julius Deluxe 1 King room has a described in-room bathroom; Guy Savoy is listed at Caesars Palace. The exact-date booking page returned a technical-error banner and no final taxed total.
  • Wynn: the exact-date operator page describes Resort King bedding/bathroom; Wing Lei is listed at Wynn. Its average nightly Cyber price excludes tax and is not used as a final seven-night quote.
  • MGM Grand: the newly reopened MGM King page describes a king bed and marble-adorned bathroom; this is not a dated room allocation or rate. Bellagio, Cosmopolitan and Crockfords also now have separately sourced room descriptions.
  • Hilton at Resorts World: a separately named King room and described bathroom are checked. Its $2,200.15 engine itemization is an observation with incomplete terms, not a fully qualified offer; do not attach this price to Crockfords.
  • Desert Rose and Cliffs at Peace Canyon: operator pages describe King-bed suites and kitchen/bath facilities. Neither has a verified door-to-door one-hour bus itinerary here; Cliffs is not presented as a convenient Strip alternative.
