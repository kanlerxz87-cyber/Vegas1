Top Las Vegas Activities & Honest Public Transit Guide

All 6 featured attractions sit within ≤45 minutes of Center Strip via the 24-hour RTC Deuce, Las Vegas Monorail, or pedestrian walkways. Grand Canyon / Hoover Dam coach excursions exceed the 1-hour public-transit threshold and are excluded.

Public Bus/Rail vs Vegas Loop Caveat: Las Vegas has no municipal subway or commuter train. Public transit consists of RTC Southern Nevada buses (24/7 Deuce + east-west Route 203 Chinatown & Route 202 Flamingo) and the 7-station Las Vegas Monorail. The 14-station Vegas Loop is a point-to-point tunnel Tesla system documented below as an optional North-Strip supplement, never conflated with public bus/rail.
