What would break this plan

  • Robuchon and é are the only irreplaceable bookings. Both are small tasting rooms on Tue–Sat; if either is full, swap the two nights (they are interchangeable).
  • Sphere has no 2027 calendar (F-16), so Friday is the flexible night.
  • Some venues publish no days at all — Sparrow + Wolf, Esther's Kitchen, Kame and Anima. Not one of them is scheduled here; all four would need a phone call first.
  • Michael Mina is closed (F-23) and appears nowhere on the plan.
  • Hours are 27 Nov 2026 observations, not 2027 schedules — the standing caveat on every page of this site.
  • Journey times are unverified (F-59). District grouping is not proof that a trip is under an hour; it is what can be supported today.
