Open-day collisions worth knowing before you choose dates:
  • Restaurant de Joël Robuchon — closed Sun & Mon
  • é by José Andrés — closed Sun & Mon, two seatings only
  • L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon — closed Tue & Wed
  • Wing Lei — closed Tue & Wed
If the trip were only 15–18 Feb (your preferred window), you would land Mon–Thu and could not eat at Robuchon or é at all. That single fact is the strongest argument for extending to the full week.

Why this order and not another

Two star-level dinners are spaced out rather than stacked, so neither is wasted on a jet-lagged evening. Chinatown sits mid-week on a night when the buses run normally and the Strip is busiest. Downtown is placed on a Friday so the Arts District is awake. Everything else is filler that can absorb a cancellation.
