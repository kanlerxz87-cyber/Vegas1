What would break this plan

  • Robuchon and é are not booked yet. Both are tasting-menu rooms with few seatings; if either is full on its night, swap them (Robuchon Tue–Sat, é Tue–Sat).
  • Sphere has no 2027 calendar. Friday 19 Feb is deliberately the flexible night (F-16).
  • Sparrow + Wolf publishes no hours (F-25). Call 702.790.2147 before relying on it; Partage is the backup on the same road.
  • Michael Mina is closed (F-23) and is not on the plan at all.
  • All prices are 2026 menus and will move before February 2027 (F-53).
