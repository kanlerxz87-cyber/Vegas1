Why there is no grand total. A realistic food budget needs the four starred venues. Two of the four have no published price, and Bib Gourmand has none by design. Any grand total would be invented. What can be said with evidence: one Guy Savoy dinner for two is $760–$1,280 before tax and tip — which is 1.6× to 2.6× the entire cheapest hotel week.

Cost-saving levers that are actually evidenced

  • Hotel choice is a real lever: $483 (Rio Masquerade) vs $2,493 (W Las Vegas) for the same 7 nights, same city. A $2,010 swing.
  • The Bib Gourmand trio is the value play — that is literally what the award means. China Poblano, Esther's Kitchen, YEN Viet Kitchen.
  • Kabuto is the best-value fine-dining on the list: $130 pp for a MICHELIN-guide omakase, versus $380+ for a starred French dinner.
  • Lunch/brunch at Mott 32 ($-modest dishes 11:00–14:30) versus its dinner service — same kitchen, lower bill.

What to add before committing money

  1. Resort fee per hotel, in writing, from the hotel's own site.
  2. Tax and gratuity assumptions (Clark County room tax; 20% service is normal at this tier).
  3. Airfare — deliberately out of scope here; no source was fetched and none will be guessed.
  4. A published price for Robuchon and é, by phone or OpenTable at booking time.
