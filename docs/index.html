Three things that change the answer

  1. The 7-night basis collides with the requested dates. The preferred window 2027-02-15 → 2027-02-18 is three nights, not seven. Seven-night totals starting on 15 Feb are used so the figures match the brief — Q-02.
  2. The "within 1 hour" ranking does not rank anything. Every hotel in Las Vegas is within an hour of all 24 restaurants, so the metric returns 24/24 for all 32 properties. A 20-minute column was added and is what actually separates them — Q-01.
  3. There is no universal cheapest week in the verified official sample. The same named Horseshoe King rate was lowest on 15–22 March among five quoted windows. Caesars Palace's standard King quote was $201/night on 15–22 February versus $317/night on 15–22 March; two other pages returned a different accessible room variant and are left blank for like-for-like comparison.
