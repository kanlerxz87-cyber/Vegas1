Read this first — three findings that change the plan
  1. Las Vegas has no subway. Your “subway or bus” requirement resolves to bus + monorail only. Every transit time on this site is a real Google Maps bus/monorail route, not a metro estimate. See Transit.
  2. One hotel will not cover all 24 restaurants inside 60 minutes. The venues split into three clusters — Strip core (12), Chinatown/Spring Mountain Rd (7) and Downtown/Arts District (2) — and only a few routes have actually been measured. Chinatown is 29–49 minutes by bus from the hotels tested, which is inside the rule; the far-south and far-west venues are unmeasured, not assumed to be close. See Transit.
  3. Your preferred dates are only 3 nights. 15–18 Feb 2027 conflicts with the 1–2 week trip length. This plan prices 15–22 Feb (7 nights) as the working proposal, starting on your preferred date. See Open Questions.
