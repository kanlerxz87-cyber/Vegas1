Transit reality check — Las Vegas has no subway

The brief's "within 1 hour by subway or bus" criterion is applied as bus + monorail + walking (Las Vegas has no subway/metro):

  • Las Vegas Monorail — 7 stations along the east side of the Strip: Sahara · Westgate · Convention Center · Harrah's/The LINQ · Flamingo/Caesars Palace · Bally's/Paris · MGM Grand. Official: lvmonorail.com (station map page failed automated fetch — verify there manually). Serves the Caesars/Flamingo/Paris/Horseshoe/MGM corridor directly.
  • RTC Transit buses — The Deuce runs the Strip 24/7 to Downtown; SDX express + route 202 serve Chinatown (Spring Mountain Rd), where 6 Michelin restaurants cluster. Official: rtcsnv.com.
  • Result: all 24 Michelin restaurants are reachable within roughly 60 minutes by bus+walk from every hotel in the table (estimate). Anima by Edo (far west), YEN Viet (Decatur) and Ping Pang Pong (Gold Coast) are the three marginal ones (est. 45–60 min). Red Rock Canyon and Hoover Dam do NOT meet the constraint (car/tour only) — flagged on the Activities page.
  • Walkability differentiates more than transit: from the Venetian/Palazzo, ~11 Michelin restaurants are within an estimated 30-minute walk; from Caesars/PH/Paris/Horseshoe/Flamingo, ~9–10. Chinatown is a short bus/rideshare from all.
Estimates flagged: walk/transit time bands are estimates from official addresses and the monorail station list, not timetable reads (RTC trip-planner results were not machine-readable this session). Use Google Maps/RTC trip planner for live routing.

Methodology & verification rules

  • Michelin status: taken from the official MICHELIN press release for the 2026 Southwest selection (Aug 26, 2026) and cross-checked with LVCVA. Star status = CURRENT guide only; 2008–09 stars are historical and called out where relevant.
  • Hotel prices: live reads of each property's own booking engine for Feb 15–22, 2027 (2 adults, 1 room), sampled 2026-11-25. Nightly = room + resort fee, excl. tax. 7-night totals = 7 × nightly (computed). Cross-checked with official flexible-date calendars. Engines that block automated reads → blank + flag.
  • Restaurant cost for 2: fixed menu × 2 = exact (e.g., Wing Lei $198.88 pp → $397.76). Where only à-la-carte items are published (Guy Savoy), totals are arithmetic ranges from linked listed prices ("computed"). Where no official price exists (Joël Robuchon, é), reported third-party figures are labeled as reported and flagged. Bib Gourmand cost = MICHELIN's own benchmark definition (~$50 pp) labeled as estimate.
  • Hours: official restaurant/venue pages only. Exceptions (holiday closures, weekly dark days) preserved in-row. Missing hours = blank + flag, never guessed.
  • Social links: only accounts linked from official pages or verified official posts are included (Gymkhana, SoulBelly, Kabuto, Neon Museum, Caesars property). Others left blank rather than guessed.
  • Nothing inferred: every cell is either sourced+linked, computed from linked values (marked "computed"), an explicitly-labeled estimate, or blank+flagged.
