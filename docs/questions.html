The four that actually block a booking

  1. Trip length. 3 nights (your preferred window) versus 1–2 weeks is a 2× to 4× difference in every other number. Nothing else can be costed until this is fixed.
  2. Event calendar. One convention week can invalidate every price quoted here.
  3. Resort fees. They are charged to every guest and are missing from all 15 totals.
  4. Robuchon / é tasting-menu prices. The biggest single expense has no published figure.

How to discover the missing information — concretely

  • Prices: run the same Booking.com search with every 7-night window from 15 Feb to 23 Mar, with the "includes taxes and fees" toggle on, and record each. Five sweeps, one spreadsheet.
  • Events: the LVCVA publishes a convention calendar; cross-check the Las Vegas Motor Speedway, T-Mobile Arena, Allegiant Stadium and the rodeo calendar (PBR / NFR are typically elsewhere in the year but must be confirmed, not assumed).
  • Resort fees: search each hotel's site for "resort fee" and record the figure and its date.
  • Menus and prices: OpenTable reservation flows for Robuchon, é and L'Atelier show the current menu price at the point of booking.
  • Hours: the official pages linked from the MICHELIN table; re-check in the week before travel because all four starred venues post temporary holiday hours.
  • Transit: complete the matrix at 6–8 PM local time, since that is when you would actually be travelling to dinner.
