A worked seven-night budget for two adults, assembled only from figures this project already verified.

What seven nights actually adds up to

This page turns the verified pieces into three totals so the choice is about money rather than adjectives. Every number is read from the same data files the other pages use, at build time — nothing here is typed by hand, and each line links to the page where its source lives.

These totals are arithmetic on captured snapshots, not quotes. They exclude Nevada room tax, resort fees where the capture did not include them, airfare, gratuities and wine. See F-29, F-30 and F-48 before treating any total as bookable.

The monorail does not reach the airport or downtown; the RTC Deuce does and runs 24 hours, which is why the RTC pass is the better fit for a plan that includes Fremont Street and Chinatown. No seven-day RTC pass was verified — the arithmetic above stacks three-day passes.

Three moves that change the total most

  1. Hotel week. The spread between the cheapest and dearest captured week for the same property is larger than any dinner on this site. Re-quote your exact dates on the property's own engine.
  2. One starred dinner. Adding a second Guy Savoy or Wing Lei night is roughly $398–$940 for two — comparable to three nights in the Value hotel.
  3. Room class. Several captured totals are for different room classes at the same hotel, so a "$300 cheaper" week can simply be a smaller room (F-13).
