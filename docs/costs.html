Cost basis — do not add incompatible numbers

A fully costed trip is still unverified. The hotel observation, menu amounts, starting activity admission and per-person transport fares are different bases, not a single reliable trip total. No exchange rate, unknown fee, tip, daily food allowance, flight or insurance amount is invented.

  • Hotel: reproduced itemization plus missing offer/eligibility terms.
  • Dining: published food menu/set subtotals for two, not tax/tip/drinks.
  • Activities: current/from admission for two, with processing fees or other exclusions.
  • Transport: product-specific couple arithmetic; not a ride plan or proof of the one-hour bus rule.

field-evidence JSON records source dates and limitations. No total is reported until every required line has a compatible verified basis.
