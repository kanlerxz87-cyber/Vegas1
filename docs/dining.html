Why no assumed Guy Savoy dinner range?

The official menu publishes individual dishes, but no complete dinner-for-two total was extracted. Earlier research constructed a range by assuming two or three dishes per adult. That calculation is retained in the raw dataset and F-64 for audit, but is not an official quote and does not enter this strict primary view.
