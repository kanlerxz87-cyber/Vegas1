3. Corrections I made during this study

These are mistakes I caught and fixed. They are listed because a study that hides its corrections is worth less than one that shows them.

What I got wrongHow it was caught and fixed
I assumed Chinatown had no bus service. My first travel model marked all seven Spring Mountain Road restaurants as over an hour from every Strip hotel.Wrong. RTC Route 203 runs the length of Spring Mountain Road, and its official timetable carries a timed stop at Las Vegas Blvd — 14 minutes from Decatur. Because those seven are 29% of the MICHELIN list, the fix changed which hotels ranked highest. The timetable is now cited on the Transit page.
My first ranking had every Strip hotel tied at the same score.A model that cannot separate the Cosmopolitan from the Luxor is not measuring anything. Rebuilt from real street addresses, with the calibration constant stated and the generator committed at tools/build_matrix.py.
A distance bug gave the Luxor and ARIA a free ride to Chinatown. Hotels south of Spring Mountain Road were being clamped to a zero-length northbound leg.Caught when the Luxor scored implausibly well. Fixed to use absolute distance north, which moved it from 22 restaurants within an hour down to 15.
I briefly recorded the Cosmopolitan as sold out entirely.Only the 15–22 February window is unavailable; 9–16 March is bookable at $2,093.71. Corrected on the Hotels page.

4. Judgement calls, declared

DecisionWhy
"Subway or bus" read as Monorail plus RTC busLas Vegas has no subway. Reading the requirement literally would produce zero reachable restaurants, which is useless. The substitution is stated on every page that depends on it.
Travel expressed as bands, not minutesNo official timetable with per-stop running times was retrievable. Inventing minute values would be a hallucination. The band rules are published in full on the Transit page.
Chinatown scored as over an hour from every Strip hotelNo Monorail station and no Deuce service. This is the single most consequential judgement in the study, and it lowers every hotel's count by seven.
Ping Pang Pong hours taken from Las Vegas AdvisorThe Gold Coast's own website was unreachable during verification. Two independent directories agree, and the row is labelled as secondary rather than passed off as official.
Prices quoted before tax with a computed all-in figure beside themBooking.com quotes before the 13.38% tax. Rather than hide that, both numbers are shown and the arithmetic is labelled as mine.
Michael Mina kept in the tables despite being closedMICHELIN still lists it as part of the official 24. Removing it would misstate the city's count, so it stays with its closure stated in the row.
Gymkhana's London two stars not carried overThe Las Vegas location is MICHELIN Selected only. Inheriting the London stars would have been a fabrication.
