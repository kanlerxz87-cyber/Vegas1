Getting around

No subway, three separate systems, and the reason the 'within one hour' ranking had to be tightened to twenty minutes.

There is no subway in Las Vegas

The brief asked for restaurants within an hour by subway or bus. Las Vegas has no metro or light rail, so three separate systems were modelled instead — and they do not share a ticket:

Correction: Chinatown is reachable by bus

An earlier version of this matrix recorded Chinatown / Spring Mountain Rd as reachable only by car or rideshare. That was wrong. RTC Route 203 runs the length of Spring Mountain Road, and its published timetable carries a timed stop at Las Vegas Blvd — the north end of the Strip, where Wynn, Encore, Resorts World and SAHARA are.

This matters more than any other single number on this site, because seven of the 24 MICHELIN listings sit on that one road: Partage, Kabuto, KAME Omakase, Lamaii, Sparrow + Wolf, Pho Ga Bac Bac and YEN Viet Kitchen. Nearly a third of the guide is on a road that turns out to have a bus.

The timetable's eastbound runs give Decatur to Las Vegas Blvd in 14 minutes. Adding a walk to the stop, a platform wait and a walk at the far end puts the north Strip at about 34 minutes from Chinatown door to door — comfortably inside the brief's one-hour limit, from a hotel with no Monorail station.

This is the only published travel time in the whole model. Every other minute figure here is an estimate built from station lists and route maps, and is labelled as one. Verify the timetable yourself: RTC Route 203 timetable (PDF).

Why the 60-minute ranking failed, and what was done instead

The brief asked to rank hotels by how many MICHELIN restaurants fall within an hour. Run literally, that number is 24 out of 24 for every single hotel in the dataset — including the ones in Henderson and Summerlin. The Strip is about four miles long and Chinatown is about three miles west of it, so an hour covers the entire city.

A metric that returns the same value for all 32 properties cannot rank them. So a 20-minute column was added, and that one does separate the field:
