Transit & the 60-minute rule

Your requirement: every restaurant and activity within one hour by subway or bus of the hotel. Measured with live Google Maps transit routing.

Correction to a premise: Las Vegas has no subway, metro or light rail. There is no underground or surface rapid-transit rail system on the Strip. What exists — and what Google Maps actually returned for every query — is:
  • RTC Deuce / Strip & Downtown Express — Regional Transportation Commission of Southern Nevada. Frequent Strip and downtown service; 24/7 on the Deuce corridor
  • RTC residential routes (201, 202, 203, 103, 104, 119) — RTC. Used to reach Spring Mountain Rd / Chinatown
  • Las Vegas Monorail — Las Vegas Monorail Company. Behind-the-Strip route, MGM Grand to Sahara; does not serve Chinatown
So “subway or bus” here means bus + monorail. Any plan claiming subway travel inside Las Vegas would be wrong.

What the measured routes already tell you

Chinatown is genuinely reachable by bus

Gold Coast → Kabuto is 29 minutes; Flamingo → Kabuto is 46 minutes; Signature/MGM Grand → Kabuto is 49 minutes. All inside the 60-minute rule, all on ordinary residential RTC routes (202, 203, 103).

The Strip core needs no bus at all

MGM Grand → Guy Savoy is a 28-minute transit estimate for a trip that is physically a walk across the Blvd overpass. Trump International → Wing Lei came back as a 15-minute walk. For Strip-internal venues, “transit time” overstates the effort.

Hotel location beats hotel quality

The cheapest hotel measured (Gold Coast, $768/7 nights) beat a hotel nearly twice its price on Chinatown access. If the Michelin list is the reason for the trip, geography is the lever.

Caveats that matter for a February trip

  • Times were captured pre-dawn on a Wednesday. Evening service on a fight-night or convention week can run longer.
  • Las Vegas summer heat is irrelevant for February, but late-night frequency is not — check the last bus before a 9:30 PM dinner at Wing Lei.
  • The monorail does not serve Chinatown or Downtown. Anything on Spring Mountain Rd is bus-only.
