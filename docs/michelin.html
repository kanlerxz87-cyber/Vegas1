What the hours actually reveal

MICHELIN dining in Las Vegas is not only a $$$$ affair

The guide's highest honours sit in the resort dining rooms, but the same guide lists kitchens where two people eat well for the price of one tasting-menu course. The hours column above is what exposes them:

  • Pho Ga Bac Bac — open 24 hours. Nothing else on this list is. A 1 AM bowl after a show is a MICHELIN-listed meal.
  • Ping Pang Pong — 10 AM to 3 AM daily. Dim sum at lunch, full menu late, and it is inside the Gold Coast, one of the cheapest hotels in this plan. Hotel and restaurant in the same building.
  • Lamaii — midnight on weekdays, 2 AM Thursday to Sunday. A serious wine programme attached to a kitchen that stays open after the Strip goes quiet.
  • Estiatorio Milos — lunch daily, plus a 3–5 PM Midday Menu. The only starred room here that will feed you at lunchtime at a lower price than its dinner.
  • Bouchon — weekday brunch from 8 AM. A Thomas Keller kitchen before the conference crowd wakes up.

Every claim above is quoted from that venue's own website in the table. Where a venue publishes no schedule, the cell says so rather than being filled in.

The one that is closed. Michael Mina at Bellagio carries a MICHELIN listing and its own page says “Monday to Sunday: Closed”. Four starred or listed restaurants in this city can be shut while the guide still lists them — a guide entry is a record of past quality, not a live opening status. Check the venue page the week you travel.

The three MICHELIN clusters in this city

1 · The Strip core (12 venues)

MGM Grand (2 starred), Cosmopolitan (1 starred + 1 Bib), Caesars Palace (1 starred), Bellagio, ARIA, Wynn, Venetian/Palazzo (4), Resorts World. This is where all four stars live. A Strip hotel puts most of this cluster inside walking distance — no bus needed.

2 · Chinatown / Spring Mountain Rd (7 venues)

Kabuto, Kame Omakase, Lamaii, Partage, Pho Ga Bac Bac, Sparrow + Wolf, and the Bib Gourmand YEN Viet Kitchen. Almost entirely Recommended rather than starred, but this is the highest density of guide-listed food in the city and it is 29–49 minutes by bus from the measured hotels.

3 · Downtown / Arts District (2 venues)

Esther's Kitchen (Bib Gourmand) and SoulBelly BBQ (Recommended), both on S Main St. Pairs naturally with the Mob Museum and the 18b Arts District on the same day.
