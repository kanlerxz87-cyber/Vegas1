Suggestions for Review

Proposed improvements to this site and to the plan. None have been implemented. Review each, choose the ones you want, and they can be built next.

How to use this page. Every item states the problem it solves, the effort, and what decision changes if you adopt it. Nothing here is applied yet.

Tier 1 — changes that would most improve a decision

S1 · Complete the 15 × 24 transit matrix (replaces guesswork with a number)

Problem: hotel ranking currently rests on 5 measured routes out of 360 possible pairs.

Proposal: route every hotel→restaurant pair at 6–8 PM local time and rebuild the ranking from the full grid. Then "restaurants within 60 minutes" becomes a real, defensible count for every hotel instead of a small-sample estimate.

Effort: high (automated routing + a manual spot-check). Decision it unlocks: which hotel to book.

S2 · Add resort fees and taxes to every hotel total

Problem: the headline 7-night figures are understated and can reorder the list.

Proposal: scrape each hotel's own fee page and show a "true 7-night total" column next to the booking-site total.

Effort: medium. Decision it unlocks: which hotel is genuinely cheapest.

S3 · Sweep every 7-night window in the allowed date range

Problem: only two date windows were compared, and they were not like-for-like.

Proposal: for a fixed shortlist of 4–5 hotels, price every 7-night window from 15 Feb to 23 Mar 2027 with taxes included, then chart the cheapest.

Effort: medium. Decision it unlocks: the dates themselves.

S4 · Verify the private-bathroom + queen-bed requirement per room

Problem: this was a hard requirement and it is confirmed for 2 of 15 hotels.

Proposal: re-run the search with both filters on, then keep only rooms whose description guarantees a specific bed type. Drop "assigned on arrival" rates entirely.

Effort: low. Decision it unlocks: eliminates half the cheap options honestly instead of optimistically.

Tier 2 — quality of life

S5 · Event calendar overlay

Import the LVCVA convention calendar and major-venue schedules into the date picker, so a traveller can see which weeks are inflated before falling in love with a price.

S6 · TikTok / Instagram verification pass

Only Kabuto has social posts verified (embedded on its own site). A manual pass per venue — checking the venue's own link-in-bio — would fill the remaining 17 rows without guessing.

S7 · Price the starred dinners via OpenTable instead of leaving them blank

OpenTable shows the current menu price at the reservation step. That is a citable, time-stamped figure and would complete the food budget.

S8 · Add a "walk vs transit" column

MGM Grand → Guy Savoy took "28 minutes by transit" for a walkable 1.2 miles. For Strip-internal venues, transit time is the wrong metric and currently makes the Strip look worse than it is.

Tier 3 — for later

S9 · A single-file PDF export of the final plan

Useful for travelling without signal, and for handing to a hotel concierge.

S10 · Saved-search / re-price alerts

Because "cheapest time to book" is unverifiable, the honest substitute is a monitored free-cancellation booking plus an alert when the same dates drop.

S11 · Accessibility and dress-code column

Four venues impose dress codes (business formal at Robuchon, upscale at é) and minor-age restrictions. That is decision-relevant and currently only appears in notes.

Site changes I would make to the design itself

  • Per-page "what changed since last snapshot" — a diff strip so a returning reader sees movement rather than re-reading everything.
  • Confidence colour on every cell — green verified / amber partial / red absent, so the page can be skimmed at a glance.
  • A printable one-pager carrying only the 12 decided facts.
  • Sticky filter bar on hotels (max price, min guest score, inside-60-min only).
