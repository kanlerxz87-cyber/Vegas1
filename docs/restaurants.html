All 24 MICHELIN Guide Las Vegas entries, with the distinction type stated explicitly on every row

Read the distinction column before the price column. MICHELIN uses four different labels and they are not interchangeable. In this city the official 2026 American Southwest selection contains exactly one two-star restaurant, three one-star restaurants, three Bib Gourmands and seventeen "Selected" listings, for 24 in total. A "Selected" entry is a recommendation, not an award. A Bib Gourmand is a value award, not a star.

Cost for two. MICHELIN does not publish prices, and most of these restaurants publish menus only as PDFs or images that cannot be read reliably. Where the operator publishes a per-person figure in text, the doubled total for two adults appears in the table, clearly marked as before tax and tip. Everywhere else the cell is blank and flagged. I did not estimate a single one.
