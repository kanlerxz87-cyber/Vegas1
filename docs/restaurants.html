What "MICHELIN" means here

Las Vegas had no MICHELIN Guide between 2010 and 2025. The 2026 Southwest selection — announced 31 August 2026 on the official michelin.com press release — is the city's first since 2009, which is why so many videos and blogs still describe restaurants as starred when they no longer are.

Watching before you go

Every starred, Bib and Recommended row carries at least one YouTube video link in the Watch column — 69 links across 24 listings — because a video shows the room, the plating and the portion size in a way a menu cannot. 17 of 24 rows also carry an Instagram link, verified one account at a time. The provenance for every row is shown below, and the blanks are explained.

What dinner for two actually costs

Only three venues publish a number, and two of them are outside the starred group:

  • Michael Mina — $295++ per person, 10-course tasting → $590++ for two, but the venue is currently closed: its own Bellagio page reads “Monday to Sunday: Closed” and labels it Temporarily closed, with no reopen date published. Treat this as a price for a service that is not running, not as a bookable dinner (F-23).
  • KAME Omakase — $500 per person, signature omakase → $1,000++ for two.
  • Anima by EDO — $85 per person tasting → $170++ for two.

The four starred rooms are all "price on request". A couple planning a stars-heavy week should expect the starred nights to be the largest line in the budget after the hotel, and should plan on reserving before booking the room — S-04.
