Method, stated plainly

  1. Distinctions were read from the official Michelin publication, not from news summaries.
  2. Hours and prices were read from each venue's own website, not from aggregators.
  3. Prices were captured live with dates and window, and the capture URL is kept with the number.
  4. Where a source could not be reached or a value was not published, the value was left blank and labelled unverified — never inferred.
  5. Where a source is an intermediary (Booking.com, Google Maps), the row says so.
Known limits of this snapshot. The MICHELIN guide pages themselves (guide.michelin.com) were blocked to this reader, so the official press release on michelin.com was used as the source of record. TikTok and Instagram were unreachable, so social links are thin. Only 5 of 360 possible transit pairs were routed. All three are recorded on Flagged for Review.
