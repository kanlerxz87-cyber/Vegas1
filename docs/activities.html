The one free thing

The Fremont Street Experience runs live music on three stages every night under the canopy, and entry is free. Put it on the same day as the Mob Museum and the Neon Museum — all three are downtown, but travel times from each hotel remain unverified; do not assume a 25–30 minute journey.
