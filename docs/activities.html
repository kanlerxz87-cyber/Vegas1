Hours and prices for most activities are blank on purpose. Museum and attraction sites change hours seasonally and many do not publish a machine-readable schedule. Rather than copy a number from a blog, the cell stays unverified. Each row links the official site — that is the one-click check.

Two activities that break your own rule

Red Rock Canyon and Hoover Dam are not reachable within 60 minutes by public transit. They are listed so the trade-off is visible: choosing them means renting a car or booking a tour, which adds cost and a day. If they stay in the plan, the hotel choice stops mattering for those days.

What would make this page far better

  • Ticket prices in writing from each official box office.
  • Seasonal hour tables captured in the same week as the trip.
  • At least one verified YouTube walkthrough per activity — currently none are attached, because searching produced mostly promotional reels rather than specific, checkable videos.
