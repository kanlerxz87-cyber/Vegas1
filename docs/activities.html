Deliberately not listed

These were checked and could not be verified, so no hours, price or link is published for them:

  • Bellagio Fountains — The official Bellagio page for the fountains returned a fetch error twice. Show times and the current schedule could not be confirmed, so no hours or links are published here. Free to watch; check https://www.bellagio.com/ on arrival.
  • Cirque du Soleil / other production shows — Booking.com listed 'Cirque Du Soleil Admission' from $158.65 per person, but that is a reseller listing with no show name, no venue and no 2027 date. Not published as fact.

The one free thing

The Fremont Street Experience runs live music on three stages every night under the canopy, and entry is free. Put it on the same day as the Mob Museum and the Neon Museum — all three are downtown, and downtown is the one zone that is a 25–30 minute trip from everywhere else.
