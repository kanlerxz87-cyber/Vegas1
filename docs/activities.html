Activities

Free and paid things to do, with the Sphere residency calendar checked against your dates

The Sphere is the headline booking and only one residency actually covers your window. Metallica's run is confirmed from 28 January to 13 March 2027. The Eagles finish on 23 January, the Backstreet Boys finish on 2 January, and John Mayer does not start until 8 April. If a Sphere show is on the list, Metallica is the only option that reliably falls inside 15 February – 23 March.

The best free thing in the city is free. The Fountains of Bellagio run every 15 minutes in the evening and cost nothing.

Fountains of Bellagio Free show · Bellagio — the lake in front of the hotel

Price: Free

No ticket required; publicly viewable from the Strip sidewalk and the Bellagio bridge.

Days: Daily

Hours: mon_fri: Every 30 minutes from 3:00 PM to 7:30 PM, then every 15 minutes from 8:00 PM to midnight; sat_sun_holidays: Every 30 minutes from 12:00 PM to 7:30 PM, then every 15 minutes from 8:00 PM to midnight

Duration: not published

Exceptions: Shows are cancelled in high winds and other inclement weather. Schedule subject to change.

High Roller Observation Wheel Paid attraction · The LINQ Promenade

Price: Adult daytime $23.50; Adult anytime $34.75; Happy Half Hour (21+, open bar) $60; Youth 4–12 $8.50 daytime / $17.50 anytime; children 3 and under free

Prices exclude tax and fees. For 2 adults the daytime cost is therefore $47.00 plus tax/fees and the anytime cost is $69.50 plus tax/fees.

Days: Daily

Hours: daily: Operates daily; the observation wheel runs through the day and evening. The official page publishes a calendar that could not be captured as a static weekly table, so exact daily opening times are left blank.

Duration: Approximately 30 minutes per rotation

Exceptions: Height 550 ft. 28 air-conditioned cabins, up to 40 people per cabin. One rotation takes about 30 minutes and boarding does not stop the wheel. Nevada residents get 20% off standard tickets in January and June before 5 PM with valid ID.
