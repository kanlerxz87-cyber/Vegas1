## Main recommendations

1. **Confirm the stay length.** Decide whether the actual trip is 3, 7 or 14 nights. Do not scale a 7-night price to 3 or 14 nights; rates and promotions may change.
2. **Compare qualifying rooms only.** Select a named king or two-queen room with private bathroom. Ask the property to confirm the bed in writing if the offer is assigned on arrival, and compare the full stay total including resort fee, taxes, deposits and cancellation terms.
3. **Do not choose a date on a market-wide savings claim.** The available samples differ by property and source. Check each hotel's exact-date engine before changing the preferred dates.
4. **Book dinners after checking current service calendars.** Verify Michelin tier, hours, closures, menu prices, reservation releases and dietary restrictions. Michael Mina is marked temporarily closed in the captured Bellagio page; do not plan it as an operating dinner without a confirmed reopening.
5. **Build activities around current hours and routes.** Recheck attraction hours, event schedules and transit service close to travel.
