## Irregularities and integration requests

1. **The 60-minute finding is not verified.** The overview says every hotel is within an hour of all 24 restaurants. `data/transit.json` describes zone-based model estimates, not dated door-to-door public bus/subway itineraries; it also includes the Monorail and Tesla-based Vegas Loop, which are not a subway or bus. A model result is not proof of the requested bus-only threshold. Integration request: avoid categorical reachability claims, leave unverified per-property counts blank until official, dated route itineraries including walking, waiting and transfers are established, and identify modes clearly. Official sources for checking: [RTC route 203 timetable](https://rtcws.rtcsnv.com/routePDF/203.pdf), [Monorail stations](https://www.lvmonorail.com/stations/), [Vegas Loop operator](https://www.boringcompany.com/projects/las-vegas-loop).
2. **Official-price-only requirement remains unmet.** The headline $564 and hotel table are third-party snapshots, not verified current hotel-issued all-in prices for a named qualifying room; some prices have inconsistent fee/tax bases. The existing [F-29/F-30 and F-39/F-40 flags](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Vegas1/flagged.html) disclose major issues, but disclosure does not transform snapshots into verified official totals. Integration request: blank values that cannot be confirmed against an official booking engine and record dated 2-adult all-in quotes and room terms when available; do not announce a cheapest booking date without a comparable date-wide sample. [Example property booking source](https://www.caesars.com/linq).
3. **High Roller official page is internally inconsistent about ticket windows.** Its tickets section says daytime 12–5 and anytime 12–close, while its FAQ says daytime 12–5:59 and anytime starts at 6. The activity row currently displays one of these alternatives without flagging the conflict. Integration request: flag the conflicting official descriptions and recheck with the operator; do not treat either as a confirmed 2027 operating timetable. [Official page](https://www.caesars.com/linq/things-to-do/attractions/high-roller).
4. **Validation limitation.** The validator checks internal HTML paths, some cross-page anchors, layout markers and counts of external URLs; it does not fetch external destinations or prove row-level attribution. The build cannot support a claim that all external links work or every row has been verified. Integration request: publish this limitation and separately verify each external source and row; record blocked or unavailable destinations rather than treating them as working.
5. **Preferred dates and costs remain decision blockers.** February 15–18 is three nights, inconsistent with the minimum seven-night stay. A seven-night stay beginning February 15 is a disclosed interpretation, not an answered preference. Travelers' budget, dietary preferences, accessibility, and real 2027 opening/availability are not established. See the existing [questions](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Vegas1/questions.html) and [flags](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Vegas1/flagged.html).
