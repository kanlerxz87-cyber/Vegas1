What verified means here

A value is published only when its own record names an official source read on the checked date. A source link does not verify every field, and an old row-level badge does not override a null. Every canonical restaurant, hotel and activity ID is covered, including incomplete rows. Source observations are recorded in the {source_file}.

“Read” describes the source-page fetch, not a comprehensive live-link guarantee. Social target pages and every hotel/archived link were not all opened. The offline validator checks HTML structure, internal links/anchors, evidence coverage and root/docs parity—not remote availability or truth.
