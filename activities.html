Activities

Things to do within roughly 1 hour of travel from the recommended hotel cluster, by bus or monorail. Each row has days/hours, exceptions, media links, and an official source.

Travel-time note

Las Vegas public transit = RTC bus + Las Vegas Monorail. There is no subway in Las Vegas. "Travel from hotel" is the estimated one-way transit time for the referenced stay area.

Notes & caveats

Caveat: Show schedules, opening hours, and any seasonal closures change. Re-verify on each activity's official site before visiting. Some shows have dark days; exceptions are noted per row.
