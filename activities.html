⏱ Sample 3-day rhythm (within 1 hour of any chosen hotel)

  • Day 1 — Strip classics: Bellagio Fountains (free, after dark) → Bellagio Conservatory (free) → dinner at a nearby Michelin star. All walkable/short ride.
  • Day 2 — Downtown: Deuce to Fremont Street Experience (free) → Mob Museum → Neon Museum (night tour). All within the 1-hour Deuce trip.
  • Day 3 — Views & immersion: High Roller (The LINQ) or STRAT SkyPod, then AREA15/Omega Mart. Monorail covers the LINQ/Strip leg.
