Flagged — Grand Canyon exceeds the 1-hour rule

The Grand Canyon West Rim is ~2.5 hours from the Strip. It is included as a bucket-list full-day option but does not satisfy the "1 hour or less" activity-travel requirement. If the 1-hour rule is strict, substitute Red Rock Canyon or Hoover Dam (both within ~45 min).
