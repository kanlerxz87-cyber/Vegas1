What does NOT fit the 1-hour transit rule

Red Rock Canyon, Valley of Fire, Hoover Dam, and the Grand Canyon are outside the 1-hour public-transit envelope from the Strip (no regular RTC service; tour-bus/shuttle or car required — exact tour durations not verified here, so they are excluded from the table rather than guessed). If a day trip matters, that's an open question: see Q7.
Pairing tip: Mob Museum + Fremont Street share one downtown trip (Deuce $8 24-h pass covers both). Chinatown dinner (Route 203) pairs with an afternoon Arts District walk on the way back north.
