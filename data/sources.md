## Notes on verification discipline
- Hotel bed types: only rooms explicitly listed with "1 king bed" are treated as satisfying the
  queen-or-larger requirement. Rooms marked "Room Type Assigned on Arrival" are flagged (bed not
  guaranteed) — see the Flagged for Review page.
- Restaurant cost figures are tasting-menu / representative per-person prices from the sources
  above; they exclude tax, gratuity and wine unless the source states otherwise. Total-for-two is
  an estimate range and is flagged where no exact current figure is published.
- Exact one-way transit times are estimates derived from the Deuce/Monorail route characteristics
  and must be confirmed with the RTC trip planner / Google Maps — flagged on the hotels page.
