How the ranking works

Las Vegas has no subway; transit = RTC buses (Deuce/SDX/local routes), the Las Vegas Monorail, and walking. Every candidate hotel reaches all 24 MICHELIN-listed restaurants within ~1 hour by bus, so raw "within 1 hour" count is 24 for all of them. The ranking therefore uses, in order: (1) number of MICHELIN-listed restaurants walkable (<25 min on foot), (2) total within 1 h transit, (3) verified 7-night price. Private bathroom + queen-or-bigger bed is a hard filter — all rows pass it (rooms listed with attached bathroom and king/2-queen options).
