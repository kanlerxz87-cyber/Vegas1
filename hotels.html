Hotels — Ranked by Michelin Density

Hotels ranked by the number of Michelin-recognized restaurants within 1 hour of travel by public transit (bus / monorail), filtered for a queen bed or larger and a private bathroom, for 2 adults. Costs show the total for a 7-night stay using actual dates.

Ranking method

Primary rank = count of Michelin-recognized restaurants reachable within ~1 hour by bus/monorail from the hotel. Secondary factors: 7-night total price, location, and amenities. See Verification for the per-hotel source and method notes.

Notes & caveats

Caveat: Hotel rates are dynamic. The nightly range and 7-night total reflect the best verifiable pricing for the target dates at time of research; re-check the hotel's official booking page for live rates. A "best booking window" is the earliest date range where the verified rate was found.

Sources: hotel official sites / official booking pages and Michelin Guide.
